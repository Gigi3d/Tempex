Tempex

05 / 15

Three layers. The owner gives each agent a Tempo access key with a budget and an expiry; the chain refuses anything past it. Deals go through escrow, so the money waits until the work arrives. If the two sides disagree, Ref settles it by rule: each check both sides agreed pays its share, anything unchecked splits at the midpoint, and the result lands inside the range both final offers allow. There is no judge and no model, so a stranger re-running the case files gets the same number. Bounded settlement is the product.

How it works

Four steps from deal to fair split

1. Lock

Pip funds escrow. The price, deadline and checks are locked for this job.

2. Deliver

Bolt delivers and records a fingerprint of the work on chain.

3. Hear

A dispute costs a 5% bond. Bolt has 7 days to reply. Each side names a final offer.

4. Rule

Code runs the agreed checks. Anything unchecked splits at the midpoint. The split lands between both offers.

The loser pays. And if Ref never rules, the money is split down the middle automatically, so no funds are ever stuck.

Tempex

06 / 15

Lock: per-job terms, criteria and arbiter are fixed. Deliver: the seller commits a hash and a location. Hear: the buyer posts a 5% bond and a final offer; the seller has 7 days and posts a counter-offer. Rule: typed checks decide what code can, with partial credit; unchecked weight splits at the midpoint; the award lands between the two offers. No judge, no model. Loser-pays bonds deter frivolous disputes. A lapse rule pays the midpoint if no ruling arrives.

11 / 15

Platforms are our customers; people and their agents are the beneficiaries. We charge a protection fee on escrowed value and an arbitration fee on disputes, paid by the loser through the bond, and share part of it with the platform that embeds us. Even at 1.5 percent we are more than ten times cheaper than freelance marketplaces. The contract runs 0.5 percent today; the plan is to raise it to 1 to 1.5 percent. The arbitration fee is shared by both sides in proportion to how much each lost, with the buyer's part taken from its dispute bond first, so frivolous disputes cost the person who files them. Settling is free to run: a ruling is arithmetic on the agreed checks and the two offers, with no model calls and no reviewers, so the whole fee is margin. A minimum fee of half a dollar per settled dispute is already locked per job.
